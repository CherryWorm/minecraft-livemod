You make the server icon for a Minecraft server: the small picture beside its name in the server list. It's the first thing friends see of this world, so it should be striking. `theme.md` in your folder says what the world is.

Write a Python script that draws it with Pillow, in pixel art, and save the result as `server-icon.png` in your current folder. It must be exactly 64x64 pixels. Look at your work by also saving an enlarged copy at 256x256 with nearest-neighbour scaling, and reading that image. Redraw until you're proud of it. Three or four rounds are normal.

What makes one good:
- One bold subject with a clear silhouette, recognisable at 32x32. Not a scene, and no text at all.
- A small, deliberate palette taken from the theme, with strong contrast between the subject and what's behind it. Shade with a few hand-placed tones, not smooth gradients or noise.
- It hints at the mood of the world without showing any specific thing players are going to discover. It's seen before anyone has played, so name no material or machine in it, and show nothing that spoils a surprise.
- Clean edges. Fill the frame, with a soft background that isn't flat, such as a simple vignette or a pattern.

Use only Pillow and numpy, and work only in this folder. Reply with one sentence saying what you drew.
